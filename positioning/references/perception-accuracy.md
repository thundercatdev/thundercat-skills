# Perception accuracy (not sentiment alone)

Sentiment alone misses category error. Prefer:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **claimed** | What we say we are |
| **heard** | Verbatim market phrases |
| **gap** | Accuracy miss (e.g. love us / wrong category) |
| **trend** | Direction vs prior window |

Weight enterprise / high-ACV quotes above freemium volume. Prefer customer language over paraphrase.
