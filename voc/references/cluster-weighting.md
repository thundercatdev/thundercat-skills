# VoC cluster weighting

Frequency alone misleads. Score themes by **value × recency × severity**.

| Input | Weight notes |
|---|---|
| Enterprise / high-ACV ask | Higher than freemium volume |
| Verbatim quote | Keep; do not paraphrase into marketing speak |
| PM brief | Top 3 themes, named customers, strongest quote — scannable in ~5 min |

Messaging updates: **current phrase** → **customer language** → **recommended replace**.
