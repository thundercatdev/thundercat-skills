# Hook quality (community / review channels)

A hook fails if a stranger could swap the company name and it still reads fine.

| Pass | Fail |
|---|---|
| Cites the exact post, review, or complaint | "Saw you were looking for tools…" |
| Maps signal → internal problem | Generic pain ("scale is hard") |
| Peer tone; one ask at most | Pitch-first or multi-CTA |

Lead table columns: **lead**, **channel**, **signal snippet**, **intent score**, **business verify note**, **CRM status**.
