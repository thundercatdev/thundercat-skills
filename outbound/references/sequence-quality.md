# Outbound sequence quality

Every touch must advance or reframe the **qualifying signal** — not escalate desperation.

| Touch role | Job |
|---|---|
| 1 | Wedge: observe the signal; peer tone |
| 2 | Proof: similar customer language / outcome |
| 3 | Reframe: cost of inaction or wrong category |
| 4+ | Soft close or new angle — never "just bumping" |

Hooks are competing theories of what they care about; label each theory explicitly.
