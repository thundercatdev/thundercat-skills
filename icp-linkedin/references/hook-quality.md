# Hook quality (LinkedIn / hiring)

Hiring spikes are leading indicators — map roles to the internal problem, not a product pitch.

| Pass | Fail |
|---|---|
| Names the hiring pattern or post specifically | "Congrats on the growth…" |
| Fit score **plus** concrete reason | Score alone ("87 fit") |
| Peer observation of org cost/friction | Vendor SEO language |

Lead table columns: **account**, **signal**, **fit score**, **fit reason**, **CRM status**, **hook draft**.
